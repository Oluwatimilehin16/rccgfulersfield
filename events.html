With the wider RCCG family

As a parish of the RCCG we join the whole church for these gatherings at Redemption City and online. Check rccg.org for exact dates.

Holy Ghost Service

First Friday of every month

The RCCG's monthly night of worship and prayer with our General Overseer, Pastor E.A. Adeboye, at Redemption City.

Annual Convention

Every August

A week of worship, teaching and prayer that brings RCCG members together from around the world at Redemption City.

Holy Ghost Congress

Every December

The RCCG's end-of-year congress of praise, the Word and miracles at Redemption City.
