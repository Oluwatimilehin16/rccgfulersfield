Who We Are

A Place Where Faith Meets Family

Fuller's Field Parish is more than a church building—it is a thriving, diverse home for seekers, believers, and families looking to anchor their lives in Jesus Christ. As a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), we are rooted in timeless biblical truths while expressing them in modern, transformative ways.

Located in Sangotedo, Lekki, our community is dedicated to helping every individual encounter the unconditional love of God, heal from past wounds, and step into their divine destiny.

12+ Years

Fostering faith, hope, and community transformation in Sangotedo.

Driven By Purpose

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a beacon of spiritual illumination where lives are redeemed, potential is unlocked, and disciples are raised to impact nations for God's kingdom.

Our Mission

To preach the uncompromising gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture authentic fellowship, empower individuals through prayer and biblical teaching, and take as many people with us to heaven as possible.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

The Trinity: We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Salvation: Salvation is a gift from God received through personal faith in Jesus Christ.

The Bible: The Holy Scriptures are fully inspired by God and serve as our supreme authority.

Holy Spirit: We believe in the baptism and ongoing empowering presence of the Holy Spirit.

Servant Leaders

Meet Our Pastoral Team

Guided by vision, integrity, and unconditional love, our leadership team is dedicated to shepherding the congregation, equipping believers, and building a thriving spiritual home for every family.

Pastor Wole & Pastor (Mrs.) Bose Obayomi

Pastor Wole and Pastor (Mrs.) Bose Obayomi serve as the Senior Pastors-in-Charge of Fuller's Field Parish. With over a decade of dedicated ministry, their heart is centered on building a warm, Christ-centered community where people from all walks of life can encounter God’s presence, heal, and flourish.

Together, they provide visionary leadership rooted in deep biblical teaching, prayer, and pastoral care. Pastor Wole brings a passionate teaching ministry focused on purpose and spiritual growth, while Pastor Bose faithfully shepherds family dynamics, women's fellowship, and community welfare initiatives.

Their unified vision is to raise grounded disciples who do not merely attend church, but actively influence their homes, workplaces, and neighborhoods with kingdom values and genuine love.

Pastor Imoh & Patience Akpan

Serving as Assistant Pastor alongside his wife Patience, Pastor Imoh Akpan plays a pivotal role in strengthening the ministry infrastructure and spiritual vitality of Fuller's Field Parish.

They work hand-in-hand to oversee daily ministry administration, pastoral care, and member assimilation. Their dedication ensures that every individual who steps through our doors finds a place of connection, mentorship, and practical spiritual guidance.

Pastor Imoh and Patience embody servant leadership, championing family stability, small group discipleship, and continuous support for our volunteers and operational teams.

Hope of Nations Leadership

The Hope of Nations Youth & Young Adults Ministry is spearheaded by a passionate, dynamic leadership team devoted to raising the next generation of Kingdom influencers, innovators, and leaders.

Recognizing the unique pressures faced by young people today, the team creates engaging environments where youth can tackle real-world questions, deepen their personal relationship with God, and discover their unique talents.

Through vibrant gatherings, career development workshops, creative arts, and outreach programs, the Hope of Nations leaders empower young adults to stand bold in their faith and positively shape culture.
