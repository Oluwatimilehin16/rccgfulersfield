Who we are

About Fuller's Field

We are a parish of The Redeemed Christian Church of God in Sangotedo, Lagos: a church family of all ages, learning to follow Jesus together.

Our story

Fuller's Field began with a simple vision: to plant a church where families along the Lekki–Epe corridor could meet God, grow in faith and find a home.

More than a decade on, that vision still shapes everything we do. We hold firmly to the truth of the Bible and the doctrines of the RCCG, and we work to express that faith in ways that make sense for life in Lagos today.

Our hope is that everyone who walks through our doors will encounter the love of God, find healing from the past and step into the purpose He has for them.

  1. 2012

    Planted

    Fuller's Field Parish begins, serving families along the Lekki–Epe corridor.

  2. 2016

    Growing ministries

    Hope of Nations launches for youth and young adults, alongside dedicated fellowships for our seniors.

  3. 2020

    Online and in the community

    Services begin streaming online while our community outreach deepens.

  4. Today

    Still growing

    Making disciples, raising leaders and shining God's light in Sangotedo and beyond.

Our vision

To be a place where lives are redeemed, potential is unlocked, and disciples are raised to impact the nations for God's kingdom.

Our mission

To preach the gospel of Jesus Christ, build real fellowship, equip people through prayer and Bible teaching, and take as many people with us to heaven as possible.

What we value

These shape how we worship, serve and treat one another.

  • The Word first

    The Bible is our foundation and final authority for faith and life.

  • Open arms

    Come as you are. Everyone is welcome here and everyone belongs.

  • Prayer and worship

    We seek God's presence with heartfelt praise and earnest prayer.

  • Every generation

    From our youngest children to our Wise Women and Wise Men, every age has a place.

What we believe

As a parish of the RCCG, we share the beliefs of the wider Redeemed Christian Church of God.

God
There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.Matthew 28:19
Salvation
Salvation is God's free gift, received by faith in Jesus Christ.Ephesians 2:8–9
The Bible
Scripture is inspired by God and is our highest authority.2 Timothy 3:16
The Holy Spirit
We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit and His power at work in believers today.Acts 1:8

Our pastors

The people who shepherd our church family through teaching, prayer and care.

Pastor Wole & Pastor (Mrs.) Bose Obayomi

Pastor-in-Charge

Pastor Wole and Pastor (Mrs.) Bose Obayomi lead Fuller's Field. For over a decade they have given themselves to building a warm, Christ-centred church where people from every walk of life can meet God, heal and grow.

Pastor Wole teaches with a passion for purpose and spiritual growth. Pastor Bose cares for families, leads the women's fellowship and oversees our welfare work in the community.

Their prayer is for a church of disciples who do not just attend on Sundays, but carry God's love into their homes, workplaces and neighbourhoods.

Pastor Imoh & Patience Akpan

Assistant Pastor

Pastor Imoh serves as Assistant Pastor alongside his wife, Patience. Together they oversee the day-to-day running of the parish, pastoral care and welcoming new members.

They make sure everyone who comes to Fuller's Field finds connection, mentoring and practical support, and they champion our small groups, our volunteers and strong families.

Hope of Nations

Youth and young adults leadership

Hope of Nations is led by a team devoted to raising the next generation of leaders and influencers for God's kingdom. They meet every Sunday from 9:30 to 11:00am.

They create spaces where young people can bring real questions, grow in their relationship with God and discover their gifts, through worship nights, career workshops, creative arts and outreach.

Part of a worldwide family

Fuller's Field is one of the many parishes of The Redeemed Christian Church of God, founded in Lagos in 1952. The RCCG is led by our General Overseer, Pastor E.A. Adeboye, whose charge to every parish is simple: make heaven, and take as many people with you as possible.
