Welcome

A Place to Discover God and Find Your Family

Fullers Field Parish is a vibrant spiritual community devoted to loving God, loving people, and doing good. As a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, we've been raising disciples and serving our community with open arms.

We live out our faith by following Jesus's example — showing up for whoever we meet, wherever we go, no religious background required. If you're searching for hope, healing, or simply a place to belong, we'd love to have you.

— Pastor Wole Obayomi
