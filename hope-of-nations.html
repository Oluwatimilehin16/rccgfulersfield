About us

Welcome to Hope of Nations

Hope of Nations is the youth and young adults ministry of Fuller's Field. We are raising the next generation of leaders and influencers for God's kingdom.

Young people face real pressures today. Hope of Nations gives them a place to bring honest questions, grow in their relationship with God and discover their gifts, with friends who are walking the same road.

  • Sunday youth serviceWorship, teaching and prayer every Sunday from 9:30am.
  • Creative artsMusic, drama and spoken word, like our Easter Praise Concert.
  • Career and life workshopsPractical sessions on work, money, relationships and purpose.
  • OutreachTaking the love of Jesus to our neighbourhood and campuses.
