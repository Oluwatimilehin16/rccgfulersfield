Workers in Training

Every worker at Fuller's Field begins with Workers in Training (WIT), the RCCG course that grounds you in the faith and prepares you to serve.

Register for WIT

Where You Can Serve

Ushering

Welcome people at the door, help them find a seat and receive the offering.

How to join
  1. Worship with us regularly at Fuller's Field.
  2. Register for Workers in Training (WIT).
  3. Tell us you'd like to join Ushering; we'll introduce you to the head of department.
  4. Start serving after training.
