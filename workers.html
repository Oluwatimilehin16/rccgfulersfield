Our departments

Tap "How to join" on any department for the steps and a quick way to email us.

Ushering

Welcome people at the door, help them find a seat and receive the offering.

How to join
  1. Be a member who worships with us regularly at Fuller's Field.
  2. Register for Workers in Training (WIT) at the parish office.
  3. Tell us you would like to join ushering; we will introduce you to the head of department.
  4. Start serving once you finish WIT and your department orientation.
