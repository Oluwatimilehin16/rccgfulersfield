Church is more than Sunday. Each ministry at Fuller's Field is a smaller family where you can grow, be known and serve.

Hope of Nations

Hope of Nations is our youth and young adults ministry, raising the next generation of leaders and influencers for God's kingdom.

Young people face real pressures today. Hope of Nations gives them a place to bring honest questions, grow in their relationship with God and discover their gifts, through worship, teaching, career workshops, creative arts and outreach.

When
Every Sunday, 9:30 – 11:00am
Where
RCCG Fuller's Field, Km 47 Lekki–Epe Expressway
Who
Teenagers, students and young adults

Children's Church

A safe, joyful place where children learn about Jesus in a way they understand, through Bible stories, songs, prayer and lots of fun.

Children's Church runs on Sundays while parents worship, so the whole family can meet God in church.

When
Sundays, during service
Who
Children of the church and visitors

Wise Women

The Wise Women are the mothers of our church: women of 60 and above who have walked with God through many seasons.

They meet for fellowship, prayer and mutual care, and they pour their wisdom and faith into the younger women and families of Fuller's Field.

Who
Women aged 60 and above

Wise Men

The Wise Men are the fathers of our church: men of 60 and above who meet for brotherhood, prayer and purpose.

They mentor younger men, stand with families in prayer and remind us all that it is never too late to serve God with joy.

Sunday school

Between our two Sunday services we break into classes to study the Bible together, using the RCCG Sunday school manual.

It is the best way to go deeper than a sermon, ask questions and get to know other members.

When
Every Sunday, 9:30 – 10:30am
Who
Adults and teenagers
Midweek

Prayer & Bible study

Digging Deep is our interactive midweek Bible study on Tuesdays. Bring your Bible, your notebook and your questions.

Faith Clinic on Thursdays is an evening of prayer for healing, help and breakthrough. On the first Thursday of every month it becomes our special Healing in His Wings service.

Tuesday
Digging Deep, 6:30 – 7:45pm
Thursday
Faith Clinic, 6:30 – 7:45pm
