No data? Use USSD

You can give from any phone, even without a smartphone or internet, using Ecobank's USSD code.

*326#

  1. Dial *326# from the phone number linked to your bank account and choose Transfer.

  2. Choose Ecobank as the receiving bank.

  3. Enter the account number for the fund you are giving to, then the amount.

  4. Check the account name reads RCCG Fuller's Field, then enter your PIN to confirm.

Menus differ slightly between banks. If you don't bank with Ecobank, dial your own bank's transfer code instead and choose Ecobank as the receiving bank.

Questions about giving

Can't find your answer? Call the parish office on 0803 303 0620 or email rccgfullersfield@gmail.com.

Which account is for my tithe?

Use the Tithe, first fruit & love offering account (0212009328) for your tithe, first fruit and regular offerings.

How do I know my transfer is safe?

Every account on this page is an official Ecobank account in the name RCCG Fuller's Field. Always check that name appears before you confirm a transfer. If anyone asks you to pay into a different account name, please contact the parish office first.

Can I get a receipt or giving statement?

Yes. Contact the parish office after you give, with the date and amount, and we will send you a giving statement for your records.
