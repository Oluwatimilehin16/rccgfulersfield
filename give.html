No Data? No Problem

Give via USSD (No Internet Needed)

You can sow your seed straight from your phone's dial pad — works on any phone, even without a smartphone or data.

Ecobank Nigeria USSD Code
*326#
1

Dial *326# on the phone line linked to your bank account, then select the Transfer option from the menu.

2

Choose Other Banks (if you don't bank with Ecobank) or Ecobank (if you already do), then enter Ecobank as the destination bank.

3

Enter the parish account number for the fund you're sowing into (see the accounts listed above), then enter the amount you wish to give.

4

Confirm the account name shown is RCCG Fuller's Field, then enter your USSD/transaction PIN to complete the transfer.

Note: USSD menus and PIN steps vary slightly by bank. If this code doesn't match your bank's menu, dial your own bank's standard transfer USSD code and select Ecobank as the receiving bank, using the account numbers above.

Good to Know

Giving FAQs

Which account should I use for my regular tithe?

Use the "First Fruit, Tithe & Love Offering" account for your weekly tithes, first fruit, and love offerings.

Is my giving secure?

Yes. All accounts are official Ecobank accounts held in the name of RCCG Fuller's Field. Always confirm the account name reads "RCCG Fuller's Field" before completing any transfer.

Can I get a giving statement or receipt?

Yes, please reach out to the parish office after giving so we can issue you a giving statement for your records.
