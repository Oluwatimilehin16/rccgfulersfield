About us

Welcome to Wise Men

The Wise Men are the fathers of Fuller's Field: men of 60 and above who meet for brotherhood, prayer and purpose.

They mentor younger men, stand with families in prayer and remind us all that it is never too late to serve God with joy.

  • BrotherhoodFriendship and fellowship among the fathers of the church.
  • PrayerInterceding for families, the church and the nation.
  • MentoringGuiding younger men in faith, work and family.
  • ServiceSupporting church programmes and special services.
