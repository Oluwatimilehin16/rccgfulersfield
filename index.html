Welcome Home

A Place to Discover God and Find Your Family

fuller's Field Parish is a vibrant spiritual community devoted to loving God, loving people, and doing good. As a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, we've been raising disciples and serving our community with open arms.

"I was glad when they said to me, 'Let us go into the house of the Lord.'"

— Psalm 122:1

We live out our faith by following Jesus's example. If you're searching for hope, healing, or simply a place to belong, we'd love to have you.
