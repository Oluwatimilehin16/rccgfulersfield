A word from our pastors

Whether this is your first time hearing about Fuller's Field or you have worshipped with us for years, we are glad you are here.

We are a family of ordinary people who love God and love one another. On any Sunday you will find grandparents and new babies, students and business owners, people who have walked with Jesus for decades and people still asking questions. There is room for you too.

Come as you are. We would love to meet you.

Pastor Wole & Pastor (Mrs.) Bose ObayomiPastor-in-Charge

When you arrive

Our ushers will meet you at the door and help you find a seat. There is no dress code: come as you are.

Bringing children

Children's church runs during service, so kids learn about Jesus in a way they understand while you worship.

Can't come in person?
